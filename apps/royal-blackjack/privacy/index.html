2. Data collection and usage

The app stores game balances, statistics, reward timing, interrupted-round recovery, credited purchase and reward identifiers, tutorial completion and preferences on your device. These records support gameplay, settings and prevention of duplicate chip credits. No separate Haller Apps account or contact details are required to play. Local game storage does not mean that analytics, advertising, purchases or Android backups stay on your device.

If you email support, your address, message and any troubleshooting or purchase information you supply are processed to answer your request. Please do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

3. Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and configuration

The reviewed app integrates Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. Analytics receives release-build gameplay and interaction events, including game opening, round actions and outcomes, virtual bets, daily rewards, store and purchase activity, settings and tutorial completion. Debug builds log custom game events locally. Firebase may also collect automatic session and usage events, app-instance or installation identifiers, device/OS and app-version details, approximate location derived from masked IP addresses, and automatic purchase events with product and price information. Actual collection depends on the SDK version, settings and consent configuration. Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces and diagnostic identifiers for troubleshooting.

Firebase Analytics may access an advertising identifier when available and permitted. Connections to providers expose connection information such as IP addresses. No separate in-app Analytics or Crashlytics collection switch was found in the reviewed version; advertising choices do not disable these services. The Firebase Remote Config SDK is included, but active remote configuration fetching was not verified. Its production use remains pending confirmation.

4. Google AdMob and rewarded advertising

Google AdMob is the rewarded-ad provider integrated in the app. Ads are requested from the Rewards screen only after you choose to watch an ad. The reviewed implementation does not automatically show ads during gameplay. AdMob can process IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), device and advertising identifiers (including advertising ID or app set ID where applicable), app interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Before requesting an ad, the app updates consent information through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), shows a form where required, and checks whether ads may be requested. This check does not necessarily mean consent to personalised ads. Where required by UMP, revisit your choices through Rewards → Ad privacy choices. Choosing a rewarded ad is separate from consent to personalisation. Ad availability depends on service configuration and your choices.

Earned rewards use locally generated receipt identifiers and a local credit ledger to avoid duplicate rewards. Pending receipts are removed after successful credit; unsuccessful credits can be retried. These receipt identifiers are separate from the advertising ID.

5. In-app purchases and Google Play Billing

Optional consumable chip purchases use Google Play Billing through RevenueCat. RevenueCat is initialised at app startup to obtain products and reconcile transactions, even before you make a purchase. The app configures a generated, pseudonymous RevenueCat app-user ID rather than a user-supplied account ID. Purchase processing can involve product and transaction identifiers, purchase history/status and technical information needed for verification and chip delivery. Credited transaction identifiers are stored locally to prevent duplicate delivery.

Google Play and your payment provider process payment credentials; the app’s purchase integration does not provide us with your complete card number. Product availability and prices depend on the store. RevenueCat’s additional SDK diagnostics are enabled for debug builds in the reviewed code.

6. Google Play Games and device permissions

The reviewed build configures Google Play Games sign-in. Google handles authentication; the app keeps signed-in status in memory and does not store a persistent player identity in its account component. Game progress remains local; sign-in is not a cloud-sync or balance-restoration guarantee. Google may handle account and authentication information under its own terms.

The app’s own manifest requests internet and vibration access, not contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Third-party SDKs may add permissions, including advertising-ID access, through their manifests. Installation and advertising identifiers are pseudonymous data, not a guarantee of anonymity.

Google services used or integrated include Firebase, AdMob/UMP, Google Play Billing and Play Games; RevenueCat supports purchases. Support email is handled through Gmail. These providers may process information outside your country under their applicable terms and privacy notices. Their roles and legal safeguards depend on the processing. No unverified transfer agreement or legal basis is represented as already in place.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s partner-app information, Firebase privacy information and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. Advertising disclosures may constitute sharing under applicable laws even without a monetary sale.

Google receives analytics, crash and advertising information through the respective SDKs. Google Play and RevenueCat handle store and transaction information for purchase processing and verification. Sending support email discloses your correspondence to the email provider. The precise controller/processor roles, any server-side integrations or mediation partners, and applicable legal grounds require confirmation against the active service configuration. The website does not claim that all SDK information remains local or that all third-party processing is anonymous.

8. Data retention and deletion

Local game and settings records remain until overwritten or removed through Android’s app-data controls or uninstalling. Clearing data can remove progress, balances and credit history. Android backup/device-transfer rules permit backup of the game preferences file; actual backup depends on your device settings. Pending ad receipts are stored separately and are excluded by the reviewed backup rules. Deleting the app does not necessarily remove backups or provider-held information.

Analytics retention depends on the Google Analytics property settings. Diagnostics, advertising and purchase information follow the relevant provider’s retention rules and applicable obligations. Support correspondence is handled under the retention approach in our existing policy: while needed to answer the request, investigate an issue or resolve a dispute, with any legally required exceptions. Exact console settings and operational deletion periods have not been verified; no fixed deadline is promised.

Send access or deletion requests to hallerappsinfo@gmail.com. Include Royal Blackjack and the type of request; do not send passwords or full card details. We may need limited information to identify the relevant records. A pseudonymous installation may not be identifiable from an email address alone, and we cannot promise that every provider record can be located or deleted. Local-data deletion does not automatically erase records held by Google or RevenueCat, and provider-controlled requests may need to be directed to those providers.

9. Children’s privacy and age restrictions

Royal Blackjack is a social casino-style game intended only for players who are at least 18 and have reached the legal gambling age in their location, whichever is higher. It is not directed to children. A content rating does not replace this eligibility rule. The reviewed app does not provide identity or age verification. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us so the report can be investigated.
