1. General Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Haller Apps ("we", "our", "us") handles data collected via our website (https://hallerapps.com). We are committed to maintaining a transparent relationship with our users.

2. Information We Collect

Our website is a static showcase and does not provide user accounts or forms that ask for personal details. Website delivery and external font requests can nevertheless involve connection information, including your IP address and user agent, processed by the hosting and font providers. If you email us, we receive the information you send.

3. Cookies and Analytics

The reviewed website code does not install analytics or advertising pixels. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and loads Inter fonts through Google Fonts. These providers receive requests needed to deliver the site and fonts and may process connection or access-log information under their own policies. We have not verified provider log-retention periods and do not promise a deletion schedule. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Google Fonts privacy information.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to external app stores (like the Google Play Store) or other third-party websites. Once you redirect from our website, their respective privacy terms and cookie settings apply.

5. Contacting Us

If you contact us via email (hallerappsinfo@gmail.com) for customer support or feedback, we will only use your email address and any shared message details to respond to your inquiry. We do not sell or share your contact details.

QR Code Scanner & PDF Scan Policy

Last Updated: 14 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

The scanning and document-processing operations described below run locally on your device. This is separate from technical and usage information processed by Firebase Analytics as described in Section 2; local scan processing is not a claim that the app collects no personal data.

The application only performs the following actions locally on your device:

  • Scanning QR codes and traditional barcodes.
  • Generating custom QR code graphics.
  • Cropping, filtering, and organizing image scans into PDF files.

These operations occur entirely on your device and are not sent to any external server.

2. Use of Firebase Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app usage. The SDK can process event-level usage information and pseudonymous identifiers before producing reports; reports being aggregated does not mean the underlying data is anonymous. Depending on app configuration, consent and device settings, information may include:

  • Device type and model.
  • Installed app version.
  • App lifecycle and interaction events, such as sessions and screen views.
  • An app-instance identifier identifying an installation of the app.
  • Advertising identifiers where collection is enabled and permitted by consent and device settings.

Pseudonymous installation and advertising identifiers may constitute personal data under applicable law. We do not describe Firebase Analytics as collecting no personal identifiers. Scan-content handling is described separately below; app-specific event payloads, consent controls and production collection settings still require verification against the QR Scanner implementation. See Google Analytics data collection information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any aggregated usage logs or other app-related metrics with third-party companies, advertising networks, or individuals outside of Google Firebase services mentioned above.

4. QR Code & Scan Content

The data contained within QR codes you scan or generate:

  • Remains strictly stored locally on your device storage.
  • Is never uploaded to any cloud infrastructure (we do not run proprietary database servers for this app).
  • Is never analyzed for profiling purposes.

5. Permissions Required

To perform its core functions, the application may request:

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to capture QR codes via the physical lens. The app does not record, upload, or transmit any video footage or camera frames.
  • Storage Access (optional): Required only if you select a picture from your local device gallery to scan for codes or choose to export PDF files to your local files storage.

No other permissions are requested or required to run the application.

6. Support Contacts

For policy inquiries or support assistance, contact us at: hallerappsinfo@gmail.com
