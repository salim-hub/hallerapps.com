How to Get Help

A few details help us understand what happened:

  • Application name and version.
  • Device model and Android version, when relevant.
  • A concise description of the issue and steps to reproduce it.
  • Screenshots, when useful; remove private information first.

Do not send passwords, complete card numbers or unnecessary sensitive personal information.
