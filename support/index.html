Royal Blackjack Support

For gameplay, virtual-chip balances, missing purchases, rewarded-ad credits or privacy requests, email hallerappsinfo@gmail.com. Include Royal Blackjack, your app version, Android device and a description of the issue.

For purchase issues, provide the relevant Google Play order reference and product. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details. For refunds, see Google Play refund information.

Royal Blackjack is for players of legal gambling age only. It offers no real-money gambling or prizes of real-world value. Virtual chips cannot be redeemed for cash or prizes. Purchases and rewarded ads are optional. Play responsibly.

If play or spending is affecting your wellbeing, take a break and seek independent support. Gambling Therapy offers information and support for people affected by gambling-related harm. It is an independent service; Haller Apps support handles app and purchase issues.
